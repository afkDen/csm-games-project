## One-sentence world signature

> “This is the world where ______.”

Describe an experience, not a color or implementation technique.

## Character truth

Which recognizable behavioral qualities from the character are being translated? Cite/record source-material research in the milestone packet when a claim is not obvious from the supplied still.

## Emotional objective

What should the visitor feel that differs from the other worlds?

## Source still(s)

List only supplied/approved source assets.

## Still-aesthetic profile

- dominant/secondary hue families
- accents
- shadow/highlight temperature
- contrast/value structure
- lighting character
- focal hierarchy / crop
- negative space
- texture / grain / optical qualities
- source-still motifs worth carrying into the world
- regions that must stay rigid

## Pastel tonal translation

How will the route preserve the still's color logic while joining the project's bright/pastel/colorful family? Which dark values are intentional contrast rather than default background language?

## Spatial depth plan

## Camera + scroll/device grammar

- dominant camera behavior
- dominant scroll/device family
- secondary device family
- progress response
- direction/velocity response where useful
- pointer/touch response
- idle/settle behavior
- transition tempo
- what must **not** move
- reverse-scroll behavior

## Transition grammar and continuity

Define the route-owned vocabulary for moving between major states. Explain how it materially differs from sibling worlds. Specify canonical state/progress ownership, property ownership, reverse behavior, retarget/cancel behavior, eligible control behavior during motion, duplicate-activation protection, and portal/game ownership handoff.

## Signature interaction

Describe the one bespoke interaction/transition that belongs to this character and why another route could not reproduce it by swapping assets/keyframes.
