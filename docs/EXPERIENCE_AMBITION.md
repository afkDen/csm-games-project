# Experience Ambition

This project should feel **free, colorful, spatial, dynamic, reactive, scroll-driven, and playable**.

## Quality principle

The primary objective is not to build the lightest possible website. The objective is to build the strongest feasible interactive experience while keeping it usable.

Performance, accessibility, reliability, and security are requirements, but they do not authorize converting the primary experience into a static marketing page.

When a problem appears:

1. reproduce it;
2. measure or visually inspect it;
3. identify the actual expensive/broken subsystem;
4. optimize secondary cost first;
5. preserve the approved still/depth/scroll/signature concept whenever a narrower correction exists.

## Required experiential signals

- still-led palette/composition/lighting rather than generic anime styling;
- pastel/colorful global tone rather than dark-by-default presentation;
- character behavior expressed through route motion and interaction;
- a continuous spatial relationship between hub, lens, and world;
- visible depth/occlusion relationships in major character still treatment;
- authored camera movement, not only DOM transforms;
- a contiguous scroll experience score for each route;
- one distinctive signature interaction and engineered peak per character;
- meaningful world response during scroll and subtle life when input rests;
- coherent reverse scrolling for scrubbed states;
- interruption-safe retargeting under rapid direction changes and eligible control input;
- materially different transition choreography between character worlds;
- substantive minigames with real state progression and responsive feedback;
- audio that reinforces space, character, interaction, and game state;
- satisfying lens handoff and return to the hub.

## Intermediate-state rule

For every major scroll/camera sequence, verify at minimum:

- entry;
- early transformation;
- midpoint;
- signature interaction;
- anticipation;
- engineered peak;
- portal formation;
- handoff / return;
- representative reverse-scroll reconstruction;
- mobile equivalents.

A beautiful first and last frame with a weak middle does not pass.

## Benchmark rule

Lighthouse and other automated scores are diagnostics. Do not remove intentional 3D, layered still treatment, games, signature interactions, or route transitions solely to raise a score unless a measured usability problem justifies a creative change after implementation-level optimizations have been attempted.
