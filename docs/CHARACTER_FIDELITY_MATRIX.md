# Character Fidelity Matrix

This document provides **interaction-direction hypotheses**, not canon summaries. Validate and refine them against the owner's supplied stills and reliable source-material references during orchestration.

The global rule from `CREATIVE_DIRECTION_CONTRACT.md` still wins: character truth is translated into a bright, pastel-forward motion language rather than automatically reproducing a dark/gritty overall site mood.

## Denji

**Behavioral qualities to express:** impulsive, direct, hungry for reward, rough-edged, eager, physical, funny, restless.

**Pastel translation:** warm cream, citrus yellow, peach/coral, punchy orange/red accents, imperfect collage energy, mechanical shapes used playfully rather than industrial-dark by default.

**Motion hypothesis:** forward drive, acceleration, mechanical rhythm, tactile overshoot, abrupt-but-readable cuts, velocity response.

**Transition grammar hypothesis:** forward penetration, interlocking/mechanical layer changes, punchy compositional cuts, and tactile compression/release. Reversal should visibly rebuild the previous composition rather than crossfade back.

**Interaction hypothesis:** user energy should matter. Faster input can increase mechanical activity, compression, shake, streaks, audio transients, or scene aggression without making the route unreadable.

**Avoid:** generic chainsaw sparks on a black background; treating aggression as constant visual noise.

## Makima

**Behavioral qualities to express:** composed, magnetic, precise, calm, controlled, quietly unsettling.

**Pastel translation:** ivory, blush, muted rose, soft warm red, pale gold, restrained peach; exceptionally clean value hierarchy.

**Motion hypothesis:** minimal movement with exact timing, centered authority, slow magnetic convergence, controlled orbit or gaze-like focus.

**Transition grammar hypothesis:** controlled convergence, concentric/lens alignment, restrained spatial reorganization, and precise state changes with almost no accidental overshoot. Reversal should feel equally deliberate rather than merely playing a tween backward.

**Interaction hypothesis:** subtle pointer proximity or scroll behavior can make the environment appear to organize itself around her rather than simply following the user's commands.

**Avoid:** generic red/black menace, frantic motion, obvious horror effects as the primary language.

## Aki

**Behavioral qualities to express:** restrained, disciplined, calm, reflective, emotionally guarded, precise.

**Pastel translation:** dusty blue, fog blue-gray, pale teal, silver, cool lavender, soft white; generous negative space.

**Motion hypothesis:** measured lateral travel, architectural depth, quiet camera rails, deliberate pauses, drifting atmosphere.

**Transition grammar hypothesis:** lateral passage, sliding architectural planes, negative-space handoffs, and calm occlusion changes. Reversal should reopen the prior spatial relationships cleanly and preserve the sense of measured travel.

**Interaction hypothesis:** low-amplitude responses with high compositional precision; the user should feel space and restraint rather than constant stimulation.

**Avoid:** reducing calmness to a static page or monochrome dark blue.

## Power

**Behavioral qualities to express:** chaotic, boastful, childish, funny, impulsive, disruptive, high-energy.

**Pastel translation:** bubblegum pink, off-white, bright red accents, peach, cyan/aqua pops, playful graphic collisions.

**Motion hypothesis:** elastic asymmetry, overshoot, collisions, reactive type, controlled disorder, velocity-sensitive secondary systems.

**Transition grammar hypothesis:** elastic collisions, snap/rebound, temporary layout destabilization, graphic pieces overtaking one another, and playful reassembly. The system may react strongly to velocity but must still retarget safely when direction changes.

**Interaction hypothesis:** the visitor's scroll speed and pointer movement can intentionally destabilize selected layout/VFX systems while preserving the central still and navigation clarity.

**Avoid:** generic gore aesthetic, random chaos with no authored rhythm, making everything move at once.

## Reze

**Behavioral qualities to express:** warm, dreamy, intimate, ambiguous, soft with an underlying destabilizing duality.

**Pastel translation:** powder blue, lilac, pearl, rose, sunset peach, watery cyan; luminous reflections and haze.

**Motion hypothesis:** continuous one-shot feeling, gentle parallax and refraction, romantic flow that gradually develops spatial or rhythmic discontinuities.

**Transition grammar hypothesis:** continuous refractive/dissolving passage, watery spatial folds, reflection swaps, and later continuity breaks that still remain deterministically reversible. Avoid the mechanical cuts or elastic collisions used by other worlds.

**Interaction hypothesis:** early input response should feel smooth and inviting; later beats can subtly break continuity, reflections, timing, or spatial assumptions before the portal/game handoff.

**Avoid:** announcing the tonal shift too early with obvious dark/horror styling.

## Cross-world rule

Do not use this matrix as a config sheet. The supplied stills can override palette and composition assumptions. Each route still needs an authored `docs/CHARACTER_WORLD_TEMPLATE.md`-based world spec using `docs/CHARACTER_WORLD_TEMPLATE.md` and a uniqueness audit against completed siblings.
